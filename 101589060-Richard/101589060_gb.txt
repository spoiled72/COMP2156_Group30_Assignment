George Brown College and Its Polytechnic Direction

George Brown College is a public college in Toronto that focuses on practical,
 career-oriented education. The college is known for hands-on learning through labs, 
 applied projects, and programs designed to prepare students for the workforce rather 
 than focusing only on theory.

Over time, George Brown has expanded to include applied bachelor’s degrees in areas 
such as technology, engineering, construction, health sciences, and business. Because 
of this expansion, the college is often described as a polytechnic, which better reflects 
the level and applied nature of its programs.

The polytechnic approach combines academic learning with real-world skills, industry 
partnerships, and applied research. This helps ensure that students graduate with 
experience using current tools and technologies that are relevant to their field.

For technology programs like Computer Systems Technology and courses such as DevOps 
for System Administration, this model is especially important. These programs rely 
heavily on hands-on labs, system configuration, and practical problem solving, which 
aligns well with the polytechnic style of education.
