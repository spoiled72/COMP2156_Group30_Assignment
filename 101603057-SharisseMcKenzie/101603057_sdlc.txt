DevOps in the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)
The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is the structured process used to plan, build, test, deploy, and maintain software.
Traditional models move through these stages step-by-step, usually saving testing and deployment for the end.
DevOps improves the SDLC by encouraging continuous collaboration between development and operations. Instead of waiting until the final phase, testing, deployment, and monitoring happen continuously through automation and CI/CD pipelines.
This helps teams catch issues early, reduce risks, and keep systems stable.
For IT professionals, learning DevOps is essential. Modern organizations rely on automation, cloud systems, and fast deployments. 
Understanding version control, CI/CD, and infrastructure management increases efficiency, reduces downtime, and prepares you for real-world environments.

In short, DevOps makes the SDLC faster, smarter, and more reliable.
