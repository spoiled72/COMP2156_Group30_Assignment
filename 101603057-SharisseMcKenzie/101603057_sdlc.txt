DevOps in the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)

The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is a structured process used to design, develop, test, and maintain software systems.
Traditional SDLC models include stages such as planning, analysis, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance.

DevOps integrates into the SDLC by promoting continuous collaboration between development and operations teams.
Instead of treating deployment and maintenance as separate phases, DevOps creates a continuous feedback loop that improves efficiency and system reliability.
