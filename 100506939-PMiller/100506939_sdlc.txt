Why is it important to learn and understand the development cycle?

Aspiring DevOps engineers can come from all sorts of backgrounds personal, professional, or educational
backgrounds - coding, project management, front end/back end development,full-stack, hardware focused, 
non-computer related fiends, etc. One thing that is universal, however, is how the software development cycle
operates in real-world, professional settings.

Being familiar with concepts like Continuous Integration (CI) and Continuous Development (CD) are foundational
for budding DevOps engineers, as they provide the framework for software development through all levels of the 
develpoment cycle. Every step of develpoment is met with opportunities for IT staff, coders, and management to 
work together to increase the seamlessness of operations.

Complete integration at all levels of development advance productivity, reduce development times, proper quality assurance,
bug-fixing and deployment of build updates, etc. When a team operates under DevOps principals, it has been proven to create 
better professional outcomes.
