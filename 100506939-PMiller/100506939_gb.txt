- George Brown College is one of many colleges located in the Greater Toronto Area. 
- George Brown was created in the 1960s and has continued to grow and develop post-secondary educational options ever since.
- George Brown has three campuses located in down Toronto   
    1. Casa Loma
    2. St. James
    3. Waterfront
- Alongside in-person course/program offerings, George Brown also provides online courses and programs that provide flexibility for all ranges of student situations.
- Another such remote learning option for students looking to upgrade or suppliment existing skills is through their Continuing Education program.
