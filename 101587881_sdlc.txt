A DevOps engineer plays a vital role in the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) by breaking down silos between development and operations teams.By implementing Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD), they ensure that code changes are automatically tested and released with minimal risk of failure.This approach not only speeds up the release cycle but also enhances the overall stability and security of the application throughout its entire lifespan. In summary, DevOps is the backbone of modern software engineering.
